Las Vegas Nightclub Costs: Budgeting for a Night Out

A practical guide to nightclub admission, VIP minimums, service charges and transportation budgets in Las Vegas.

What a nightclub budget should include

Plan for more than admission. Your night-out budget may include entry tickets, transportation, drinks, gratuities, coat or bag check, and any minimum spend tied to a reservation. Costs vary dramatically by date, performer, group size and venue.

Understand table minimums

A table minimum is typically a required spend on qualifying purchases rather than an all-inclusive final price. Ask for a written quote explaining whether tax, service charges, gratuity, and other fees are added on top. Confirm guest capacity and whether the quoted minimum changes on high-demand nights.

Compare ticketed entry with table service

Tickets are often simpler for individuals or small groups who do not need a dedicated seating area. Table service can be convenient for groups, but splitting the final bill should be agreed upon in advance. A guest list may have cutoffs or admission conditions and should not be treated as guaranteed entry.

Build a realistic plan

Choose the event first, confirm the venue's official policies, then estimate transportation and a buffer for unexpected expenses. Keep confirmation emails and review cancellation terms before paying. Never assume that a celebrity-hosted event includes personal access to the celebrity.

Policies, pricing and event details can change. Always confirm current terms directly with official venues and organizers before paying.
