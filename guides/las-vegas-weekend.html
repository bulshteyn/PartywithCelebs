Plan a Las Vegas Nightlife Weekend

Start with your dates and preferred music or entertainment style, then check venue calendars before choosing a hotel.

Book around confirmed events

Look for official schedules and purchase admission through recognized sellers. If you're reserving a table, get the minimum spend and total estimated fees in writing.

Allow time to move around

Travel between Strip properties can take longer than expected during major events. Leave extra time for lines, rideshares and entry cutoffs.

Check the details

Confirm dress codes, age requirements, identification rules and cancellation policies. For group bookings, make sure the reservation reflects your full party size.

Information is general and should be checked against current venue policies.
