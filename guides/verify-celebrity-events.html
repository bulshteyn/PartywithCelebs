How to Verify a Celebrity Event Before You Book

Celebrity appearances and hosted events can change. Before purchasing, confirm that the event is listed by the official venue, promoter, ticket seller or artist.

Cross-check the details

Compare the event date, venue address, age restrictions, lineup and entry conditions across official sources. Beware of copied graphics and social accounts with misleading names.

Read the terms

Look for refund and cancellation policies, and confirm whether admission guarantees anything beyond entry. A host appearance is not the same as a meet-and-greet.

Pay carefully

Prefer established ticketing channels and payment methods with purchase protections. Avoid sending irreversible payments to unknown individuals.
