VIP Table Minimum Spend Explained: What the Quote Really Means

Learn how nightclub VIP minimum spends work and which extra fees to ask about.

Minimum spend versus total bill

A minimum spend usually means you agree to purchase a specified amount of qualifying food or beverages. It does not always include taxes, service charges or gratuity. Ask for a written estimate of the complete amount due.

What counts toward the minimum

Some venues count only certain menu items or categories. Confirm whether bottled water, mixers, food or premium upgrades qualify. Clarify what happens if your group spends less than the minimum.

Deposits and payment timing

A deposit may hold the reservation or be credited toward the bill. Ask when the balance is due, which payment methods are accepted and whether the deposit is refundable if plans change.

Guest counts and placement

Check the maximum number of guests, whether additional guests cost extra and whether the table location is guaranteed. Ask whether admission is included for every guest.

How to compare offers

Request comparable written quotes for the same date, guest count and table category. Compare the estimated total, cancellation policy and included services rather than focusing on the headline minimum alone.

Policies, prices and availability can change. Confirm current terms with the venue, organizer or authorized seller before purchasing.
