New York City VIP Table Service: What to Know Before Booking

Understand NYC VIP table reservations, deposits, minimum spends and guest limits before paying.

Compare like-for-like quotes

Ask for offers for the same date, group size and seating category. Prices can vary substantially by event and venue, so avoid treating one quote as a citywide benchmark.

Understand the minimum

Confirm what qualifies toward the food-and-beverage minimum and what extra taxes, gratuities, service charges or fees may apply.

Check guest capacity and entry

Ask how many guests are included, whether additional guests require approval or payment and whether everyone must arrive together. Admission remains subject to venue policies.

Review the cancellation policy

Find out when deposits become nonrefundable, what happens if the event changes and whether the reservation can be transferred or rescheduled.

Keep written confirmation

Save the reservation contact, receipt, arrival time, table category and full terms. Share the relevant information with the group before the event.

Venue policies, events, prices and schedules change. Confirm current details with official organizers before purchasing.
