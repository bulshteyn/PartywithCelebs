Los Angeles Celebrity Events: Access and Etiquette

Los Angeles hosts premieres, brand activations, public performances and private industry events. Many high-profile events are invitation-only, so a venue location or celebrity rumor is not evidence that the public can attend.

How to plan

For public events, use official event pages and authorized ticket sellers. Respect private venues and personal boundaries; do not trespass, follow individuals, or attempt to enter restricted areas. For red-carpet events, check whether public viewing areas are permitted and whether tickets grant admission to the event itself.

Before you book

Avoid third-party sellers promising private access or personal introductions without verifiable authorization.
