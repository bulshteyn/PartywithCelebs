Chicago VIP Table Service: Booking and Budget Questions

Compare Chicago nightclub VIP table reservations, minimum spending requirements and guest policies.

Start with the event and headcount

Table pricing and availability can change depending on the night, featured entertainment and group size. Confirm the exact event and whether all guests are eligible for entry.

Ask for the total expected bill

A quoted minimum spend may exclude taxes, service charges and gratuities. Request a written breakdown and confirm which purchases count toward the minimum.

Clarify the table location

Ask whether the seating category or specific location is guaranteed, what happens if the venue changes the layout and how many guests the reservation admits.

Check the deposit terms

Confirm payment deadlines, cancellation conditions, refunds, rescheduling options and whether a deposit is applied to the final bill.

Prepare your group

Share arrival instructions, identification requirements, dress code and the booking contact with everyone. If a guest is late, ask whether they can still enter under the reservation.

Venue policies, events, prices and schedules change. Confirm current details with official organizers before purchasing.
